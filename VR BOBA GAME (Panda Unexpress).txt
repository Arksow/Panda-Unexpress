VR BOBA GAME (Panda Unexpress)

Main Gameplay Loop:

Player types their name and goes into the game -> Customers come in waves with their order and timings the player has to complete the order by -> player makes the orders and earns money (points and currency they can use in main menu to upgrade stuff/buy more ingredients) -> amount of customers and difficulty of order increases with waves (endless) -> player fails 3 orders and game ends, leaderboard is shown.



Unique mechanics:

Players start with serving only one recipe, other recipes can be discovered by the player through their own experimentation

Scooping mechanic for toppings and ice

Mixing mechanic for liquid bases(the different recipes)

Slot sugar machine where players can choose the sugar levels and add sugar syrup/honey into the drink
